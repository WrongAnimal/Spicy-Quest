**"A people without a throne know only one law the law of the blade."**  
– Orc proverb, [[Doombringer Clan]]

---

In the dark folds of the galaxy, where light dares not linger and order crumbles into dust, there rises once every decade a storm of blood, fire, and fury.

It is no festival.  
No ceremony.  
No game.

It is war.  
A reckoning.  
A cycle of vengeance and ascension.

It is the **Blood Cycle**.

---

### Origins of the Blood Cycle

The Blood Cycle was born in the ashes of rebellion. Long ago, when the Orcs shattered the chains of an ancient empire that sought to enslave them, they emerged free but leaderless.

Without a crown or a central order, their civilization splintered into countless Clans, each driven by power, pride, and primal strength.

To prevent eternal civil war, the Elders of the first age forged a brutal pact:  
Every ten years, all Clans would cast aside their alliances and turn their weapons inward, in a galaxy-wide war of succession.

Only one would emerge the **Overlord**, ruler of all Orcs.

---

### The Ritual of Blood

As the Cycle begins, the galaxy becomes a crucible of violence.

All pacts are broken.  
Kin slays kin.  
Blood runs across shattered moons, derelict warships, and the poisoned winds of dead planets.

There are no rules.  
No mercy.  
No negotiations.

The strongest survives. The rest become legend.

The last warrior standing is crowned not with gold, but with silence for no challenger remains to speak against them.

They are not chosen.  
They are **earned**.  
They are the storm incarnate the new **Overlord**.

---

### Echoes in the Galaxy

To outsiders, the Blood Cycle is monstrous a barbaric spectacle of chaos.  
To the Orcs, it is sacred.

It is how their people remember who they are.  
It is how they unify through blood.

Once an Overlord is crowned, the fractured Clans rally behind them for five years of unified war. A purge. A campaign. A galactic reckoning.

Their most hated targets?  
The **[[Dark Elves]]** beautiful, prideful, and despised.  
Sometimes, the Orcs set their sights elsewhere: human colonies, trader worlds, even forgotten gods.

When the Blood Cycle ends, war always begins.

---

### The Cycle Never Ends

The **Blood Cycle** is more than a tradition.  
It is the pulse of a people.  
It is fire in their veins.  
It is proof that might is not just law it is destiny.

As long as Orc hearts beat,  
as long as Clans rise and fall,  
as long as stars burn…

**The Blood Cycle will return.**  
And with it, the galaxy shall bleed.
