For centuries, the Orcs have waged a merciless war against the **[[Dark Elves]]** a conflict of beauty against brutality, order against instinct, light against shadow.

To the Orcs, it is The Hunt.  
To the Dark Elves, it is The Green Plague.

Once the Overlord is crowned, the Orcs unite under one banner for five years of unrelenting conquest. Cities fall. Forests burn. Blood flows in rivers. There is no mercy, no honor only carnage.

But their darkest truth lies beneath the battlefield:  
Orc women are barren cursed, or perhaps a product of ancient genetic decay.  
To preserve their kind, the Orcs seek fertile vessels.

And among all the races of the galaxy, none are more coveted than **Dark Elf women** strong enough to survive childbirth, beautiful enough to be claimed as prizes.

So they are hunted. Taken. Used.
