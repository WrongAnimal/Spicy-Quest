### **Biography:**

**Lya Serrano** was the younger sister of [[Camila]] and the daughter of [[Isabel]] and [[Alejandro]]. From an early age, she displayed a deep fascination for the ocean and all forms of aquatic life. She began studying marine biology with the dream of one day working on the planet [[Thalassara]] a world covered almost entirely by water at a remote research outpost operated by the Nyxari. There, she hoped to explore the mysteries of the deep alongside other marine scientists.

Unlike [[Camila]], Lya never aspired to become a soldier, even if she looked up to her older sister in many ways. [[Camila]] was her closest confidante the one she turned to in times of doubt, seeking guidance and comfort. Lya was a diligent, kind-hearted, and uncomplicated student. In her spare time, she helped her mother in the flower shop, earning a little pocket money and enjoying the quiet, simple moments of life.

On the day of the devastating attack, Lya was on her way to university she never returned. Since then, she has been listed as missing. Like so many others, the government has officially declared her dead.

But to [[Camila]], Lya is more than just another name among the lost. She lives on in every quiet memory, in every smile held back a symbol of what was lost, yet never truly forgotten.
