### **Biography:**

**Keshia Wonder** is the current President of **[[Viripila]]**, serving her first term in office. Despite having held the position for only two months, she was immediately confronted with a massive invasion a crisis she has managed with remarkable resilience and clarity under pressure.

## Background and Youth

Keshia grew up in the suburbs of **Lemos**, an industrial metropolis on [[Viripila]]. Her parents were respected members of the working class:

- **Her father** worked as a **spaceship technician** in an orbital repair dock.
    
- **Her mother** was a **system coordinator for terraforming simulations**, a demanding yet grounded role focused on digitally modeling and optimizing planetary environments.
    

Keshia was not an exceptional student. Her grades were average, but she stood out through her **persuasiveness, outspoken nature, and unyielding determination**. She was elected **class representative**, where she gained her first political experience.


---

## Political Career

At the age of **17**, she founded the youth party **“New Voice of Viripila” (NSV)**, a movement advocating for transparency and social justice. Initially ridiculed, her party steadily gained momentum, particularly in urban areas.

In the years that followed:

- She overcame several internal party crises, lost elections, and endured setbacks
    
- Yet she grew from her failures, built networks, and earned public trust with her authenticity
    
- At **27**, she was elected to the local senate
    
- At **30**, she took over leadership of the now-established NSV
    
- At **34**, she ran for President and won
    

She prevailed against several established candidates – not through political scheming, but with a mix of **boldness, humility, and relatable communication**.


---

## Personal Life

Keshia is **unmarried** and has no children. Her career has always taken priority; she is known for being **dedicated to her work**, but not aloof. She lives modestly, avoids political extravagance, and remains true to her style even as President.

During the invasion, Keshia demonstrates unexpected leadership qualities. Despite her lack of military experience, she coordinates the defense of the capital with notable composure. Her decision to rely on external aid – including the Captain – earns her both respect and criticism. Nonetheless, she remains resolute, even as pressure mounts within her cabinet.
