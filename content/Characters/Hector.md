### **Biography:**

Hector is a **[[Nudsimia]]** born on the planet **[[Viripila]]** to a pair of successful merchants. His childhood lacked for nothing until tragedy struck. At age 14, while accompanying his parents on a business trip to **[[Sharrhut]]**, the capital world of the [[Novari]], their lives were forever changed. A failed coup erupted against the ruling **[[Tut-Ura family]]**, and during the chaos, government forces opened fire. Over 3,000 [[Novari]] citizens died that day. Hector’s parents were among them.

The event would later become known across the galaxy as **[[Tut-Ura Dotcha Seran]]**

Stranded, penniless, and orphaned, Hector survived the streets of [[Sharrhut]] until a member of the influential **[[Kascada family]]** experts in information trade, blackmail, and covert operations took him in. From that day, Hector lived under their banner.

---
### Military

At age 40, Hector was arrested during a failed [[Kascada family]] operation. To avoid a lengthy prison sentence, he was forcibly conscripted into the army where he met a 16-year-old recruit who would one day become the **[[Captain]]**. The two formed an unbreakable bond during the brutal years of war and have remained best friends for over 14 years.

---
### Personal Life

At age 30, Hector married **[[Yala Dekoto]]**, an [[O'Landras]] from the lush planet **[[Garamanam]]**. Their passionate relationship eventually burned out after ten years. Despite the divorce, the two remain close and share a strange but stable friendship.

(Yala runs a shop of her own, but more about her is reserved for her dedicated codex entry.)

---

### Present Day: The _Gloria_

Hector now commands his own heavily modified warship: the **[[Gloria]]**. A juggernaut in deep space, the [[Gloria]] is **his pride and joy** a symbol of everything he’s conquered and survived.

At the front of the ship, **a monstrous [[Giga]]** is chained an untamable, wild creature named **[[Gaja]]**. Gigas are **not tameable, not loyal, and certainly not pets**. They act purely on instinct violent, raw, and emotionless. They do not follow orders. They **follow hunger, bloodlust, and primal force**.

No one knows how Hector captured [[Gaja]]. Some whisper he tricked her. Others say he beat her in combat. But the result is the same: [[Gaja]] now protects the Gloria like a beast guarding its lair.

> "She’s not a pet. She’s not a partner. She’s a fucking nightmare on a leash." Hector, smugly, during a trade negotiation.

Aboard the Gloria, **Yala Dekoto** also runs a store fully independent from Hector. Though they cross paths daily, their relationship today is purely professional.
