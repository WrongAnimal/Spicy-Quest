### **Biography:**

**Ina O’Conner** is the personal assistant and secretary to President [[Keshia Wonder]]. She manages all organizational matters, appointments, and communications everything that the President doesn’t have time for. Thanks to Ina’s efficiency, the presidential office runs like a finely tuned engine.

Ina is known for her **cheerful and warm personality**. She is quick to smile, always has a witty comment at the ready, and her **sense of humor** often helps lighten even the most stressful situations. Staff and visitors alike appreciate her positive energy.

But her greatest passion lies elsewhere: **books**. In a world where printed literature has become rare, Ina finds joy in the tactile experience of reading real, physical books. Her favorite genre is **romantic fiction**, and she is especially enamored with the works of an old Viripilan author, Jared Melvyn, whose heartfelt love stories she reads over and over.

She collects antique editions and is often found during her lunch breaks in the archives, curled up with a novel in one hand and a cup of herbal tea in the other. Her colleagues sometimes tease her for it, but Ina wears the label of “hopeless romantic” with pride.

## Personal Background

Ina grew up in a state-run orphanage and was adopted at the age of eight by an older couple who gave her a stable and loving home. She often refers to them as her **true family**, and their support was instrumental in her development.

She studied administrative sciences and took on various clerical roles before working in local government offices. Her exceptional skills and dedication eventually caught the attention of Keshia Wonder’s campaign team.

When Keshia won the election, Ina was offered the role of executive assistant – a position she accepted without hesitation.

## During the Invasion

On the day of the invasion, Ina was present in the **presidential bunker** alongside Keshia. There, she witnessed the devastation unfold in real time. Tragically, her adoptive parents were among the victims of the initial bombardments.

Despite the emotional toll, Ina continued to support Keshia through the crisis, never once showing signs of breakdown in public. Her inner strength and loyalty have earned her quiet admiration across the entire government staff.
