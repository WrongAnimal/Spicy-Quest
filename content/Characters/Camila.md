### **Biography:**

Camila was born on the planet [[Viripila]] a world built on the ashes of war, a place of rebuilding, of hope, and of deep scars left by the Great [[Novari]] War. Her father, a highly respected naval admiral in the planetary defense unit, was more than just a role model to her he was a living hero. As a child, she often followed him to the garrison, eyes sparkling as she watched the soldiers train. She swore that one day, she would wear the uniform herself not just to make him proud, but to protect her people.

At only twelve years old, she enrolled in the military cadet academy a school reserved for the most gifted and disciplined youth. Camila stood out not only for her sense of duty but also for her relentless ambition. She excelled in all subjects, but it was in close combat where she truly shined. While others focused on advanced ranged weaponry, Camila dedicated herself to mastering the art of hand-to-hand combat. Her technique was not only efficient but graceful combining precision with raw power. It wasn’t long before she entered tournaments, defeating even older and more seasoned soldiers. Her achievements didn't go unnoticed: at 19, she became the youngest officer in Viripila's history an honor still recorded in the military's annals.

But her greatest triumph would soon become her greatest nightmare.

Following her promotion, she decided to celebrate with a few comrades in the capital the first time in years she'd allowed herself to relax, to laugh, to drink, to feel young. That night, the sky turned red. A hostile artificial intelligence  a remnant of forgotten wars  struck the planet with devastating force. Decades of defense and training were wiped out within 24 hours. Camila fought without rest, evacuating civilians, pulling the wounded from the rubble but it wasn’t enough.

When the assault ended, everything had changed.

Her mother [[Isabel]] and younger sister, [[Lya]], perished when a bunker collapsed. Her father [[Alejandro]] disappeared no body was found, no clues remained. Some believe he was among the male civilians abducted by the AI for an unknown purpose.

Scarred by guilt and grief but unbroken, Camila volunteered for any mission that might bring answers or even hope of finding her father. When [[President Wonder]] announced a perilous expedition into deep space to respond to a distress signal from an unknown world, Camila was among the first to enlist.

That’s when she met the [[Captain]] a stranger, yet someone whose resolve she immediately recognized. Someone she believed might uncover the truth. Since then, she has followed him across the stars as a soldier, a guardian… and perhaps, one day, something more.
