### **Biography:**

**Alejandro Serrano** was a man forged from discipline and duty a naval admiral in the planetary defense unit of [[Viripila]], known for his unwavering loyalty and commanding presence. He was a figure of strength and structure, whose stern demeanor rarely wavered. Yet, beneath the uniform and the commanding voice was a heart that only his wife [[Isabel]] and his two daughters, [[Camila]] and [[Lya]], truly knew how to reach. For them, Alejandro was not the admiral he was a husband, a father, a protector.

His family meant everything to him. No accolade or victory could compare to the pride he felt when [[Camila]] chose to serve her people. When he received the news that she had become the youngest officer in [[Nudsimia]] history, he made sure to deliver the message to her personally a rare and emotional moment that revealed the man behind the medal.

[[Camila]] had invited him to celebrate the achievement with the family, and he had promised to join. But duty called once more an urgent matter summoned him back to central command. He never made it to the celebration.

When the attack struck, Alejandro was already deployed with his fleet, standing at the front lines to defend [[Viripila]] with all the might he could muster. As the skies burned and the enemy overwhelmed the planet’s defenses, contact with his fleet was lost. Like millions of others, Alejandro Serrano disappeared in the chaos.

He is now officially listed as missing in action. The authorities consider the chances of survival to be exceedingly slim. 
But for [[Camila]], he remains more than just a name on a list. He is the fire that fuels her resolve, the unshakable spirit behind her every action a silent compass guiding her through the cold and fractured galaxy.
