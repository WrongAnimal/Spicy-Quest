### **Biography:**

Althea Visiron  was born into splendor. The only child of [[King Tadel the Fifth]] and [[Queen Oldura]], she was raised in unimaginable luxury: elite tutors, designer gowns, gemstone-laced tiaras, and golden cutlery before she even knew how to read. But within this perfect shell grew a child who never learned compassion, empathy, or restraint.

From an early age, Althea was known for her temper, entitlement, and lack of respect toward anyone even her own parents. Her tutors described her as “brilliant, but unbearable.” Servants feared her, the nobles whispered about her behind closed doors, and the common folk dreaded the day she would ascend to the throne.

---

### **Rise to Power**

Though her official reign began after her parents’ death, Althea had acted like a queen her entire life. Her word was law long before it was backed by crown or court.

Friendless and impossible to please, Althea was gifted a [[Kuranari]] slave by her parents in a desperate attempt to soothe her moods. The slave young, submissive, and genetically modified to be compliant was **castrated** before being delivered to her. As the royals put it: _“A loyal dog must never bite.”_ For Althea, the [[Kuranari]] was not a person, but a possession her pet, her toy, her silent reflection.

---

### **The Day of the Attack**

When the assault on the palace began, Althea vanished into the family’s emergency bunker an underground fortress sealed with heavy security protocols. Her parents, however, didn’t survive the attack.

To this day, no one knows why [[King Tadel the Fifth]] and [[Queen Oldura]] never made it inside. Some say they were caught too late. Others whisper that **Althea was the first to reach the vault… and closed the door behind her**.  
No proof. No witnesses.  
Just rumors.

Or... is there more to it?
