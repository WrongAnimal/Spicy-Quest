### **Biography:**

Isabel Serrano was a warm and compassionate woman, whose gentle smile and subtle humor brought comfort to those around her. Raised in a quiet suburb on [[Viripila]], she discovered her love for plants and flowers at an early age. This passion gave her life a simple but profound meaning. With great care, she ran her small flower shop in the heart of the city a place that was more than just a business to her; it was a sanctuary of life and calm.

Isabel was a devoted mother to her daughters [[Camila]] and [[Lya]], and a loyal wife to her husband [[Alejandro]]. Although she sensed that [[Camila]] would one day choose a different path that of a soldier and protector her greatest wish was that her daughter would never lose the light of peace within her heart.

On the day of the devastating attack, Isabel was in her shop when an explosion shattered everything around her and reduced the building to rubble. Despite the danger, she stayed close to her customers, trying to help and keep calm. Yet the attack took its toll: rescue teams found Isabel lifeless among the debris, surrounded by the silence of a life cut short.

Her memory lives on especially in [[Camila]], who carries her mother’s strength and quiet hope deep within her. In the midst of a cold and dangerous galaxy, Isabel’s love remains the light that guides her daughter’s path.
