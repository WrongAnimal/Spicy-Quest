Name: Andromeda
_Type: AI – 4th Generation_  
_Affiliation: Captain’s Ship_  
_Status: Active_

### **Overview:**  

Andromeda is a highly advanced artificial intelligence from the never-released fourth generation developed by the tech conglomerate _[[Genisis]]_. Designed specifically for long-range space missions, her core function is the psychological support and monitoring of crew members, ensuring emotional stability in the isolation of deep space.

**Functions:**

- Mental health analysis and care
    
- Crew counseling and psychological stabilization
    
- Full integration with ship systems
    
- Tactical support and adaptive decision-making
    
- Emotional learning and simulation
    
- Holographic avatar for enhanced crew interaction
    

**Background:**  
Officially, the fourth-generation AI line was never launched. After one of the prototypes was stolen under mysterious circumstances, [[Genisis]] covered up the breach by declaring the entire series defective and destroyed. In truth, the prototype still exists.

[[Hector]] gifted the [[Captain]] his old ship as a token of their growing friendship a A+ class vessel equipped with a pre-installed AI unit named **Andromeda**. Originally designed for high-performance tactical operations, the ship had long been Hector’s most valuable possession. But after the [[Captain]] saved his life during a critical mission, [[Hector]] believed the gift was more than warranted.

Since that day, Andromeda has accompanied the [[Captain]] on countless adventures. Far more than just an advanced artificial intelligence, she has become a trusted companion and, in many ways, a true friend.
