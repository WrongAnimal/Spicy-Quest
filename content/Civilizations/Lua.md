The **Lua** were a highly advanced civilization that once ruled [[Ring 1]] of the [[Ring Galaxies]]. Known for their scientific curiosity and technological genius, they became the guardians of the innermost ring and its powerful energy source, the [[Virellion]].

They were admired across the rings for their wisdom, beauty, and innovation. Many believed them to be descendants of the [[Aethar]], although this was never confirmed.

## Culture and Society

- Focused on knowledge, discovery, and control of natural forces
- Lived in floating cities powered by ambient Virellion energy
- Viewed the universe as a system to be understood and perfected

## The Experiment

Driven by their desire to understand Virellion, the Lua attempted to merge their own essence with the energy field. Their goal was to transcend physical limitations and create a new form of existence.

Unknowingly, their experiments tore the delicate protective field created by the [[Aethar]], weakening the ancient barrier that kept the [[World Devourer]] away from the Ring Galaxies.

## Fall of the Lua

Once the breach occurred, the World Devourer entered [[Ring 1]] and began consuming worlds. The Lua fought back with all their knowledge and power, but their own technology failed due to the collapsing laws of physics caused by the devourer’s presence.

In a final sacrifice, the Lua created an unknown weapon and sealed Ring 1, destroying all [[Ring Gates]] leading outward. They vanished along with their civilization, leaving only ruins and silence behind.
