### **History and Origin**

In the blackened reaches of the galaxy, beyond navigational maps and diplomatic concern, roams a force of chaos, fury, and relentless hunger:  
**The Orcs.**

Where other civilizations build thrones from laws and temples of faith, the Orcs forge their legacy in blood, steel, and war.  
They have no central planet.  
No palaces.  
No peace.

Their homes are colossal warships, world-breakers that prowl through the stars like predators, hunting for glory, spoils, and future generations.

---

**The Cycle of Blood**

Orc society is a fragmented mass of rival clans brutal, unyielding, and treacherous even among their own. Yet every ten years, silence falls as the call of the **[[Blood Cycle]]** echoes across the void.

Then begins a war like no other:  
Clans turn on clans in a galaxy-shaking contest of might. A trial by fire to crown the next Overlord.  
Only the strongest, smartest, and most savage may stand atop the mountain of corpses that follows.

The current ruler of this eternal tempest is **[[Kar-Gush]] the Butcher**, a berserker warlord whose rise to power left three rival clanlords in pieces.

---

**The Seed of Chaos**

Orcs possess a singular, terrifying gift one unmatched by any known species:  
**They can breed with any intelligent lifeform.**

What others call abomination, they call salvation.  
Their offspring are wild, savage, monstrous
But alive.

The Orcs scatter their legacy like wildfire, seeding entire worlds with bloodline and wrath.  
Every hybrid is a testament to their will to survive, and a weapon against the future.

---

**Despised, Yet Unforgotten**

Despite their overwhelming numbers and brutal prowess, the Orcs remain outcasts.  
They hold no seats in the [[High Council]].  
They have no diplomats.  
No voice in the halls of power.

They are not considered a civilization 
They are a **cataclysm**.

And yet…  
Their legend spreads.  
With every child stolen.  
With every moon set ablaze.

For the Orcs believe in no gods.  
No laws.  
No destiny.

Only the next war.  
And the strength to win it.
