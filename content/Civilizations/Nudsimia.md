### **History and Origin**

The Nudsimia are a people born from the ashes of the great Novari War. Displaced and in search of a new home, they found refuge on the planet Viripila. There, far from the conflicts and power struggles of the galaxy, they began to build a new community. Rising from the ruins of their past, the Nudsimia forged a future for themselves and grew into a flourishing civilization.

The first settlements were small, but over time their numbers and strength grew. Their culture thrived and their society blossomed as they began to utilize the wealth of their planet and establish a prosperous trading system. Their societal structure was based on unity and mutual support.

**Society and Culture**

The Nudsimia are a people who strongly value unity, respect, and perseverance. Through their experiences as war refugees, they have learned to survive together in difficult times and support one another. Their culture is shaped by the memory of war and the hope for a better future.

Despite their relatively modest influence in the galaxy, the Nudsimia have managed to build a strong trading network. Their technology and economy are well-developed, particularly in resource management and sustainable energy. Their society is organized and guided by a wisdom born from their past as refugees and their present position as a free people.

**Political Structure**

The Nudsimia are led by President [[President Wonder]], who governs the nation with wisdom and caution. Keshia Wonder is a charismatic and respected leader who guides her people through difficult times. She is a symbol of hope and survival, embodying the spirit of the Nudsimia.

**Present Day**

Today, the Nudsimia stand as a free people in a galaxy full of intrigue and power struggles. Their trade relationships extend across the galaxy, but they do not have a permanent place in the High Council. Nevertheless, their influence remains through their connections with other peoples and their role as beacons of hope. The Nudsimia are recognized by their allies as a nation of peace and hope, even though they remain cautious in political conflicts.
