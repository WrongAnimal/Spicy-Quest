The Aethar are one of the oldest known civilizations of the ancient galaxy. They existed long before the creation of the [[Ring Galaxies]] and are worshipped by many cultures as creator gods, although their existence has never been conclusively proven.

They were a race of pure intelligence, whose understanding of matter, time, and energy surpassed everything modern civilizations can achieve. Their technology was not mechanical, but completely in tune with the flow of the universe.

It is said that the Aethar could shape matter with their minds, stabilize galaxies with their will, and even create new physical laws when needed. They were not numerous, but their influence permeated all life.

## Nature and Beliefs

The Aethar saw the universe as a living entity. They did not believe in ownership or power, but in harmony between all things. Every one of their actions served balance. They were guardians of order, not rulers of chaos.

Many religions today, especially within the inner [[Ring 1]], worship them as beings of light or celestial mentors.

## Creation of the Rings

As darkness approached, the Aethar created the [[Ring Galaxies]] – three massive artificial structures designed to preserve life from the coming collapse. These rings act as the ark of the universe.

The Aethar bound their very essence to the structure of the rings and ascended into pure energy, now known as [[Virellion]]. Their presence continues to exist through this energy field.
