**Title:** _The Aztecs – Solar Rulers of the Ancient Past_  
**Category:** Lost Civilizations  
**Status:** Extinct

---

In the hidden annals of history, over 7000 years ago, the Aztecs ruled over the galaxy as we know it. They claimed their power and authority as a direct gift from _Tonatiuh_, the Sun God. Through his divine might, the Aztecs were said to possess the ability to decide over life and death and to shape the universe according to their will.

Legends and the accounts of survivors speak of a massive beam of light that erupted from the sky, obliterating ships, armies, and entire planets. It is said that the Aztecs were in direct communion with their god and could call upon his favor at any time. Yet the true nature of this divine connection remains a mystery to this day a riddle that continues to inspire the imagination of cultures across the galaxy.

The Aztecs were invincible. Their rule went unchallenged until the day an inscription was discovered, stating that the Aztecs had lost their favor with their god. The exact circumstances of this loss remain unknown, but it is believed to have marked the beginning of their downfall.

The other civilizations of the galaxy, long oppressed under Aztec dominance, saw their chance. In a desperate alliance, they rose up and launched a massive war against the once-mighty rulers. Eventually, the united forces succeeded in defeating the Aztecs and bringing their tyranny to an end.

The last known leader of the Aztecs was **Chieftain Xiuhcoatlund**. He met his end during the [[Great War]], when he was beheaded by **[[Naira]]**, the leader of the **[[Light Elves]]**. With his death, the final resistance of the Aztecs collapsed, and their reign came to a definitive end.

Yet the legends and mysteries of their rule endured through the millennia, and the Aztecs remain one of the most fascinating and enigmatic chapters in galactic history.
