### **History and Origin**

**Living in Harmony with Nature**  
The O'Landras reside on the lush, tropical world of [[Garamanam]]. They have consciously renounced war and trade, choosing instead a life of balance with nature. Their values center around what can be felt, touched, and experienced not theorized or owned.

**Hospitality as a Virtue** 
Hospitality is sacred to the O'Landras. They welcome all who respect nature as they do. Outsiders are not seen as threats, but as curious travelers, possible allies, and students of the natural way.

**Healing Arts & Natural Medicine**  
Renowned throughout the galaxy, the O'Landras are masters of herbal medicine. Their deep understanding of native plants and natural remedies makes them sought-after healers, offering cures where even technology falls short.

**Artistic Soul**  
Peaceful but expressive, the O'Landras are gifted artisans. Their woodcarvings, made from exotic trees, and vivid artworks capturing the vibrant essence of their world, are admired across the stars.

**Spiritual Bond with Nature**  
The O'Landras maintain a deep spiritual connection to their planet. Through rituals and ceremonies, they honor the Earth, the spirits, and the cycles of life. These traditions are vital to their cultural identity and a daily part of life.

---

### Politics & Society

**No Role in the High Council**  
The O'Landras do not hold a seat in the [[High Council]] and refrain from political affairs. Their worldview rejects galactic power struggles in favor of self-sufficiency and peaceful coexistence.

**Culturally Isolated, Not Socially**  
By refusing trade and warfare and turning their backs on politics, the O'Landras live in gentle isolation. However, they remain socially open, continuing to share their hospitality and principles with other species who cross their path.

---

### Survival & Challenges

**Peaceful Resilience**  
Amidst the chaos and wars of the galaxy, the O'Landras have carved out a quiet existence. Their rejection of conflict and devotion to natural balance has allowed them to thrive without influence over galactic politics. Still, their wisdom and culture are respected far and wide.

**Stewards of Nature's Gifts**  
Their greatest strength lies in their ability to harness the raw resources of [[Garamanam]]. Through healing and art, they offer beauty and aid to a galaxy overrun by machines and earning them quiet admiration and reverence.
