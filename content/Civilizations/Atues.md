### **History and Origin**

In the midst of a devastating war, [[Tadel the First]], a man of intellect and courage, stood at the forefront. As a high-ranking officer and skilled advisor, he had access to the critical resources and technologies that were essential for the war effort. However, while battles raged and the fate of the galaxy hung in the balance, Tadel also devised plans that would bring him personal wealth.

Using his unique position and clever maneuvers, Tadel was not only able to push back the enemy but also accumulate his own fortune. Whether through shrewd investments, lucrative bribes, or risky smuggling operations, his wealth grew steadily as others fought to survive.

When the war finally ended and the galaxy began to recover from its wreckage, Tadel faced a new challenge: What to do with his immense wealth? The answer was clear and obvious: he would found a kingdom.

With the glory of his acquired wealth and the wisdom of a strategist, Tadel rose to the top of his own realm, which came to be known as the Atues. Under his leadership, the kingdom flourished.

**Society and Culture**

The Atues are a people who were shaped by the trials of war and the wealth that followed. Their culture is founded on strength, strategy, and resourcefulness. Having risen from the ashes of a galaxy-wide conflict, the Atues value their hard-earned power and wealth. They place a great emphasis on trade, politics, and maintaining control over their resources.

Despite their affluence and power, the Atues have never gained a seat in the [[High Council]]. Their influence has been denied, but they continue to govern their realm with wisdom and determination. Their leadership is firm, and their people are proud of their independence and status.

**Political Structure**

The Atues are ruled by [[Queen Althea]], a descendant of Tadel the First. She holds the throne with grace and intelligence, upholding the legacy of her forebear. Her leadership is characterized by pragmatism and diplomacy, making the Atues a force to be reckoned with in the galaxy.

**Present Day**

Today, the Atues are part of the Free Peoples of the galaxy. They engage in trade across the stars, amassing wealth and influence through their successful mercantile ventures. Though they have no voice in the [[High Council]], their impact is still felt across the galaxy. The Atues are a kingdom of power, wealth, and unwavering resolve, and their name will forever echo in the annals of history.
