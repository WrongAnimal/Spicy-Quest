### **History and Origin**

Once, they stood alongside the [[Light Elves]], sharing a glorious heritage and forming the backbone of the Elven military. But where the [[Light Elves]] sought enlightenment and peace, the Dark Elves embraced strength, discipline, and the art of war.

As the [[Light Elves]] laid down their weapons and retreated into radiant sanctuaries of faith, the Dark Elves were left behind outcasts of a new age, misunderstood and unwanted. For 400 years, they endured exile and poverty, cast aside by those they once called kin.

Yet from this abandonment rose a new empire. In the shadows of the galaxy, they forged their destiny on the dark and unforgiving world of [[Kandolara]] a planet of violet skies, obsidian cliffs, and frozen resolve. It was there that the Dark Elves rediscovered their true purpose: beauty through strength, order through power, and pride through pain.

[[Warlady Sillyana the Iron]] united their fractured houses. Under her rule, a new nation was born one that rejects compromise, ignores diplomacy, and lives by the sword and the law of retribution.

**Endless Conflict with the Orcs**

In the eastern fringe of the galaxy, an ancient war rages without end:  
**Dark Elves versus [[Orc]] Clans.**  
The Elves call them [[The Green Plague]] vile, grotesque creatures whose mere presence is an affront to all that is noble, refined, and sacred. To the Dark Elves, Orcs are not enemies they are abominations.

Dark Elves value beauty as a reflection of the soul. Flawless skin, sculpted strength, and a radiant posture are signs of inner discipline. To be beautiful is to be virtuous. As such, the Dark Elves are regarded as one of the most stunningly graceful species in the galaxy proud, elegant, and physically perfect.

This very perfection, however, has made them targets.

[[Orc]] females are naturally infertile. To preserve their bloodlines, male Orcs seek out strong and viable breeding partners and among all species, Dark Elf women are deemed the most desirable.  
Strong enough to carry [[Orc]] offspring, beautiful enough to satisfy base desires to the Orcs, they are the ideal vessels.  
To the Dark Elves, this is the ultimate violation.  
A defilement beyond forgiveness.  
A declaration of war.

**Faith and Belief**

The Dark Elves worship only one of the [[Divine Four]]:  
**Varnor – God of Justice and Punishment.**

They reject mercy.  
They do not believe in forgiveness.  
To them, all sin must be punished. Strength is a burden and those who fail, must fall. Their faith offers no comfort, only judgment.

**Their Place in the Galaxy**

Isolated. Feared. Unyielding.

The Dark Elves do not seek alliance.  
They are not represented in the [[High Council]].  
They owe nothing to anyone.  
And yet, they endure.

They remember.  
The betrayal of the [[Light Elves]].  
The hunger of the Orcs.  
The silence of the stars.

And they prepare.  
For the next war.  
For the next trial.  
For the final reckoning.
