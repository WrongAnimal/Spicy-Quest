### **History and Origin**

In the forgotten mists of time, in an era when the stars were still young and the galaxy full of wonders, one of the oldest races flourished the Elves. Mentally and physically beyond compare, they became a source of wisdom and strength that made the universe shine.

Six thousand years ago, the Elves developed a martial art unmatched by any other. Their intelligence and physical prowess far exceeded the average, and they claimed victory over the mighty Aztecs. Led by the divine [[Naira]], the Elves led their armies to triumph, defeating the Aztec forces by beheading [[King Xiuhcoatlund]] almost single-handedly... according to legend.

After liberating the galaxy, the Elves founded a new galactic government to restore peace and harmony. However, after a thousand years of peace, a disease called greed began to spread, poisoning the government and infiltrating the entire galaxy. Driven by the [[Novari]], the government became corrupt and undermined.

The Elves saw the disaster coming but chose not to intervene, feeling indifferent to the affairs of the galaxy. Yet, soon a new war broke out, and the galaxy descended into chaos and suffering. The war lasted six centuries, and the galaxy was left a mere shadow of its former self. Many civilizations lost their lives, and even more lost their homes. The Elves, too, suffered as the imbalance in the galaxy affected their religious life.

**Ilmare and the [[Divine Four]]**

Then, a young Elven woman named [[Ilmare]] rose against the [[Novari]]. She gathered all the Elves under the banner of the [[Divine Four]] to free the galaxy from sin and suffering. It was [[Ilmare]] who had the courage and determination to bring an end to the [[Novari]]. According to legend, she was the rebirth of the divine [[Naira]], possessing the power to destroy her enemies, liberate the people, and lead the galaxy into a new era of peace almost single-handedly... according to legend

**Culture and Beliefs**

The Light Elfen are deeply spiritual and uphold a belief system that revolves around the [[Divine Four]], powerful deities who guide their actions. The Elves' culture is rich in wisdom, artistry, and reverence for the divine, with a strong focus on maintaining balance in both the physical and spiritual worlds.

**Political Structure**

The Light Elfen are led by [[High Priestess Ronda]], a direct descendant of the divine [[Naira]]. She governs with wisdom, compassion, and authority, ensuring that the Light Elfen remain a force for peace and balance in the galaxy. The Light Elfen are a member of the [[High Council]] and share the responsibility of determining the fate of the galaxy with the other members.

**Present Day**

Today, the Light Elfen are a proud and powerful civilization. Their influence extends across the galaxy, and their voice carries weight in the decisions made by the [[High Council]]. While they remain neutral in most galactic affairs, they stand ready to protect the galaxy from any threat that would disrupt the balance they have worked so hard to maintain. Their wisdom and unwavering commitment to peace make them a beacon of hope in a galaxy often plagued by turmoil.

(El’Luari = The Blessed by Light or Children of the Light)
