### **History and Origin**

The Aelyari are among the most widespread and culturally influential species in the known galaxy. With a population exceeding 320,000,000 individuals, they inhabit numerous worlds and are considered a driving force within the [[High Council]], to which they contribute not only their voice but also their military power.

**Society:** 
Aelyari society is strictly hierarchical and rooted in millennia-old traditions. At its pinnacle stands the elected Imperator [[Valerion Tarsis]], a lifelong ruler whose authority surpasses even that of the powerful Senate. The Senate drafts legislation, which the Imperator may approve or veto.

Beneath them are the **Patricians**, a wealthy elite who serve as commanders and advisors. Below them are the **Citizens**, the working populace. To attain full citizenship and with it the legal right to reproduce all individuals must complete a mandatory ten-year service in [[the C.U.M.]] Army. Only 20 to 40% survive this rigorous term, a statistic considered acceptable due to the species’ high birth rate and rapid reproduction cycle.

**Culture:**  
The Aelyari culture revolves around aesthetics, sensuality, and physical perfection. Nudity is not taboo, but rather a proud expression of personal freedom and cultural identity. Public baths, massage rituals, dance ceremonies, and large-scale orgiastic festivals are integral to daily life, serving both social bonding and spiritual cleansing. Gender plays no significant role in partner selection physical attraction and mutual respect are the core values of intimacy.

**Homeworld:**  
The ancestral home of the Aelyari is **Aurelia Magna** a world of radiant beauty, featuring endless marble cities, aromatic gardens, and rolling hills laced with thermal springs. It is the seat of the imperial family and the sacred chambers of the Senate.

**Military Role:**  
The Aelyari form the primary military force responsible for defending the galaxy. Their army is efficient, disciplined, and highly advanced in technology. While Patricians rarely enter the battlefield, common soldiers form the backbone of galactic order. Many missions are carried out independently by Aelyari forces not only in defense but also in enforcement of galactic law.
