### ### **History and Origin**

Amidst the endless reaches of the cosmos where star-born nebulae glow and gravity hums with alien melodies exists a people as diverse as they are captivating: the Furrys known collectively as the **Kuranari**.  
Half legend, half reality beings of animalistic features, yet gifted with minds that command reverence even from the oldest artificial intelligences.

The Kuranari are regarded as the guardians of galactic knowledge. No other civilization gathers, organizes, and preserves information with such precision. Their data archives stretch back millennia, deep into the earliest known ages of the galaxy.  
And yet, despite their command of information, they seek neither dominion nor expansion. Their strength lies in understanding, not in control.

---

**[[Lufo Hasto]] – First Chancellor of the Galaxy**  
Kuranari history is shaped by a figure whose legacy still echoes through the data-streams of the stars: Lufo Hasto, founder of the [[G-Log]] and the first Galactic Chancellor.  
In an era of turmoil, he was the one who united the fractured factions of the Galaxy.

Under his leadership, the [[Drexarii]] were driven back, the [[Orc Alliance]] was dismantled, and humanity was saved from extinction. He was an architect of peace a diplomat with the soul of a strategist.  
For 125 years, he guided the fate of the galaxy until his death at the age of 178. But his ideals live on, encoded into every current of the [[G-Log]].

---

**The Four Noble Houses**  
Kuranari society is divided into four princely houses not as rivals, but as living expressions of a people that sees strength in diversity.

---

- **House Eritus**  
    Proud, direct, and battle-hardened. The Furrys of House Eritus are biologically enhanced with lightning reflexes and unshakable resolve. Their forces are considered elite among galactic coalitions, masters of close combat and tactical warfare.
    

---

- **House Valtus**  
    Gentle, sensitive, empathic. The Furrys of Valtus are not warriors of muscle, but of heart. They sense emotional shifts, excel as healers, diplomats, and artists. Often underestimated but those who once laughed seldom forget the consequences.
    

---

- **House Namus**  
    Grace and pride, courage and curiosity. House Namus breeds the heroes of Kuranari culture. Its members are famed explorers, daring pilots, and silver-tongued envoys. Many iconic figures of the galactic stage come from Namus their reputation often arriving lightyears ahead of them.
    

---

- **House Kutus**  
    Quiet, analytical, visionary. Kutus is the cradle of science the birthplace of the greatest innovations, including the legendary [[G-Log]], the digital backbone of interstellar communication. Here, theories are born, AIs are crafted, and the boundaries of technology are redrawn daily.
    

---

**Role in the Galactic Council**  
Today, the Kuranari are not just the archivists of the galaxy they are its advisors, its analysts, and its memory. Their seat on the [[High Council]] is undisputed, and their voice often decisive.  
They may not wield swords but their words carry the weight of fleets.

---

**Final Word**  
The Kuranari are no conquerors. No tyrants. No loudspeakers of power.  
And yet, few civilizations have shaped the galaxy as profoundly as they have.  
Their strength lies in the diversity of their Houses.  
Their power in the depth of their wisdom.  
And their greatest weapon is the understanding of what was, what is, and what may come.

In a galaxy filled with conflict and chaos, the Kuranari are not just a people
They are a foundation.
