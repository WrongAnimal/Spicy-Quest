**Virellion** is the name given to the pure, living energy that flows through the [[Ring Galaxies]], especially concentrated in [[Ring 1]]. It is not merely power, but the refined essence of the ancient race known as the [[Aethar]].

To the inhabitants of the rings, Virellion was once thought to be a natural phenomenon a perfect energy field that allowed machines to work without fuel and life to flourish in harmony. In truth, it is the spiritual residue of the Aethar, left behind when they ascended into a higher form of existence.

## Properties

- **Self-sustaining**: Virellion recharges machines and systems automatically.
- **Living**: The energy seems to react to emotion, thought, or spiritual presence.
- **Sacred**: Some civilizations viewed it as divine or god-given.

## The Lua's Mistake

The [[Lua]], scientists and guardians of Ring 1, discovered Virellion and attempted to harness its full potential. They believed it could be used to rewrite the laws of physics, to create a utopia free of decay or death.

But their experiments pierced the delicate energy fabric unknowingly maintained by the Aethar. This act weakened the invisible protective barrier that shielded the Ring Galaxies from the [[World Devourer]].

## Aftermath

The breach in the Virellion field allowed the darkness to enter. The Lua paid the ultimate price for their ambition, and Ring 1 was consumed by chaos.

Today, Virellion is unstable. It still flows through the rings, but its purity is compromised. Few dare to interact with it, for fear of triggering another catastrophe.
