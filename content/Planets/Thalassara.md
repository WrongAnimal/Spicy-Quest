### **Codex Entry: Thalassara**

Thalassara is a rare ocean planet located on the outer edge of the [[Nyxari]] Cluster, with 99% of its surface covered by water. There are no natural landmasses only a few man-made research platforms and floating stations offer any solid ground. The atmosphere is breathable but characterized by heavy humidity and dense, cloud-covered skies.

To date, only around a third of the planet has been systematically explored. Its oceans reach depths exceeding 30,000 meters in many regions, and the complex interplay of pressure systems, deep currents, and electromagnetic anomalies make detailed mapping extremely difficult. Some areas are known as **“silent zones”** regions where instruments fail, signals vanish, and communication breaks down. [[Nyxari]] scientists refer to these as the “Whisper Depths.”

Thalassara’s oceans are home to an immense variety of unknown lifeforms ranging from microscopic luminous plankton to colossal, leaf-like creatures drifting through the abyss with kilometer-long tendrils. Some species exhibit bioluminescent patterns that appear responsive to sound frequencies. No signs of intelligent life have been found so far.

Some researchers have reported sightings of massive, seemingly mobile structures on the ocean floor that appear too geometric to be natural. Others speculate that an ancient cataclysm may have submerged entire landmasses, leaving remnants of long-lost civilizations hidden beneath the waves. There are even rumors of the so-called “Heart of Thalassara” a massive energy core deep within the planet’s interior.

Despite its mysteries, Thalassara is considered a prime destination for marine biologists, xenologists, and planetary archaeologists. Access is strictly regulated to protect its delicate ecosystems and prevent unauthorized interference. Those who are granted entry find themselves in a realm of silence and wonder a deep, endless world full of beauty, secrets, and hidden dangers.
