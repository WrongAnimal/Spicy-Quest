#### Overview:  
Klaro-Melko stands as one of the most advanced and most controversial corporations in the galactic food sector. Unlike traditional dairy producers, Klaro-Melko doesn’t bottle classic milk but rather an exclusive, biologically active beverage extracted from male essence.

### Production & Collection:

Harvesting is performed through a safe, hormone-regulated extraction process directly from donors of various species. In high-tech facilities, male genitalia are systematically "milked" under strictly controlled medical and hygienic conditions. The resulting product is valued as an energy booster, stimulant, and even as an aphrodisiac lifestyle drink.

Klaro-Melko products are featured at interstellar expos, in elite nightclubs, and among extreme athletes as a premium performance enhancer.

---
### Product Diversity:

Because taste, texture, and nutritional values vary from species to species, Klaro-Melko offers a wide range of essence:

---
- ### **Human Essence**  
**Name:** Core Classic  
**Flavor:** Mildly salty with a nutty aftertaste  
**Texture:** Thin, clear, lightly foaming  
**Special Trait:** Ideal for everyday consumption; frequently used in energy blends  

---
- ### **[[Kuranari]] Essence**  
**Name:** Wild Pulse  
**Flavor:** Earthy and spiced, often with animalic musk notes  
**Texture:** Creamy, slightly oily  
**Special Trait:** Stimulating effect on many non-furry species  

---
- ### **[[Light Elves]] Essence**  
**Name:** Celestial Flow  
**Flavor:** Gently sweet with notes of lavender, starbloom, and vanilla  
**Texture:** Smooth as glass, with a shimmering glow  
**Special Trait:** Contains rare photonic particles found only in elven biology highly priced, often used in rituals  

---
- ### **[[Dark Elves]] Essence**  
**Name:** Shadow Serum  
**Flavor:** Bold and metallic, with hints of blood orange and licorice  
**Texture:** Dense, nearly syrup-like  
**Special Trait:** Strongly aphrodisiac; used in dark rituals as a power enhancer  

---
- ### **[[Aelyari]] Essence**  
**Name:** Azure Purity  
**Flavor:** Crisp, minty-fresh with traces of iron and grape flavor
**Texture:** Cold on the tongue, slightly gelatinous  
**Special Trait:** Exceptionally pure identical in quality across all batches. Its molecular harmony remains unexplained and is revered in medical and ceremonial circles.  

---
- ### **[[Drexarii]] Essence**
**Name:** Prime Instinct  
**Flavor:** Fiery and intense, with chili-like heat  
**Texture:** Steaming, viscous, almost sticky  
**Special Trait:** Contains enzymes unique to monstrous physiologies known to cause hallucinations in several species  

---

- ### **[[Giga]] Essence**  
**Name:** Titan Stream  
**Flavor:** Heavy, metallic-salty with deep earthy tones  
**Texture:** Thick and creamy, similar to a dense protein shake  
**Special Trait:** Extremely rich in testosterone-like compounds and macronutrients officially recognized as a nutritional supplement

### Competition & Legal Disputes:

For decades, Klaro-Melko has been locked in fierce competition with [[Pero-Milk]], a corporation that specializes in female-sourced dairy. This corporate rivalry has led to protracted legal disputes and public scandals.

The result: a court-mandated division of the galaxy into _influence zones_. Each company is restricted to production and sales within its respective domain. However, rumors persist of secret black-market trades and border infringements...
