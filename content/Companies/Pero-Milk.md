#### Overview:
Pero-Milk is a well-known beverage company based in the western sector of the galaxy, specializing in the collection and refinement of milk from a variety of species. Its slogan: _“A taste for every being.”_

### Production & Collection:

Milk is extracted directly from the breasts of willing workers in a process considered both humane and comfortable. Modern milking technology ensures a safe and efficient experience. Pero-Milk's production facilities are among the most advanced in the galaxy, featuring AI-monitored quality control and automated bottling lines.

Visitors can tour these facilities and even sample select high-end products during tastings.

---

### Product Diversity:

Because taste, texture, and nutritional values vary from species to species, Pero-Milk offers a wide range of milks:

---

- ### **Human Milk**

**Name:** _Classic Harmony_  
**Flavor:** Gentle, mildly sweet, with a smooth, creamy finish.  
**Texture:** Silky and uniform.  
**Special Trait:** Exceptionally compatible with all known species; often used as a base for custom blends and flavored drinks.  

---

- ### **[[Kuranari]] Milk**

**Name:** _Velvet Wild_  
**Flavor:** Fruity-nutty with a subtle spiced kick. Depending on the origin of the Furry donor, undertones may include berries, cinnamon, or woody notes.  
**Texture:** Slightly thicker, velvety on the tongue.  
**Special Trait:** Known to have a mild aphrodisiac effect on certain species.  

---

- ### **[[Light Elves]] Milk**

**Name:** _Lumina Essence_  
**Flavor:** Ethereal and floral, with soft vanilla-honey tones. Emits a faint bluish glow in complete darkness.  
**Texture:** Almost weightless, melts instantly in the mouth.  
**Special Trait:** Regarded as a sacred beverage. Known for its calming and regenerative properties.  

---

- ### **[[Dark Elves]] Milk**

**Name:** _Nocturne Silk_  
**Flavor:** Bittersweet with notes of dark chocolate, smoke, and aromatic herbs.  
**Texture:** Thick and rich, similar to a fine liqueur.  
**Special Trait:** Naturally energizing. Marketed as a noble "night elixir."  

---
- ### **[[Aelyari]] Milk** 

**Name:** _Blue Harmony_
**Flavor:** Delicately sweet with a cool, crystalline undertone. Often described as "blooming frost" a gentle fusion of floral essence and serene clarity.
**Texture:** Silky and almost weightless. Leaves a faint shimmering sensation on the tongue.
**Special Trait:** Aelyari milk is prized for its exceptionally stable nutrient structure. It contains rare enzymes believed to have a soothing effect on the nervous system.

---

- ### **[[Giga]] Milk**

**Name:** _Titan Vitalis_  
**Flavor:** Strong, iron-rich, with deep salty undertones. Considerably more intense than other milk types.  
**Texture:** Dense and creamy, resembling a heavy protein shake.  
**Special Trait:** Packed with macronutrients and essential minerals. Advertised as a “power source” for the body.  

---

### Competition & Legal Disputes:

Pero-Milk has been locked in a bitter legal war with its main rival, [[Klaro-Melko]]. After years of lawsuits and sabotage, both sides agreed to divide the galaxy into zones of operation, never to cross into each other’s territories.
