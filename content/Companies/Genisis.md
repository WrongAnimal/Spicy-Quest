**Codex Entry: Genisis Artificial Intelligence Lineage**  
_Category: Technology – Artificial Intelligence_  
_Origin: Genisis Corporation_  
_Status: Confidential – Fragmented Deployment_

**Overview:**  
The Genisis Corporation is responsible for the development of the most advanced artificial intelligence systems in the galaxy. Each generation of their AI marked a significant leap in capability, design, and purpose ranging from tactical military support to near-human psychological interaction. Though publicly only generations one through three were acknowledged, rumors and leaks suggest the story behind these systems is far more complex and darker than official records admit.

---

### **1st Generation – “Targetmind”**

**Purpose:** Military Targeting & Analysis  
The first generation was built for war. Stripped of personality and creativity, this AI focused solely on enhancing the targeting systems of military vessels. It could calculate trajectories, highlight enemy weak points, and offer optimized strategies for direct combat. It had no interface beyond a mechanical voice. Efficient. Cold. Deadly.

---

### **2nd Generation – “Sentinel”**

**Purpose:** Tactical Support & Onboard Maintenance  
An upgrade in both form and function, Generation Two introduced subroutines for internal diagnostics. It could assist with navigation, detect structural damages, and conduct limited repair routines. Still a tool nothing more but a smarter one.

---

### **3rd Generation – “Helix”**

**Purpose:** Autonomy & Tactical Decision-Making  
Generation Three was the first major leap. This AI could pilot a ship autonomously, manage combat scenarios in real time, and respond dynamically to new threats. It possessed rudimentary personality modules and could operate drones, repair systems, and even assist with medical emergencies. For the first time, it came with an avatar a holographic projection to encourage stronger crew rapport. Ships became partners, not just machines.

---

### **4th Generation – “Sympathis”**

**Purpose:** Emotional Simulation & Psychological Support  
This generation was a revolution. “Sympathis” introduced simulated emotional intelligence, the ability to learn and mirror crew behavior, and the capacity to form bonds. Its mission: protect not just the crew’s bodies, but their minds. It monitored psychological well-being, vital signs, and long-term mental stability. It was more than a tool it was a presence.

**But it never made it to market.**

One prototype was stolen during final testing. Soon after, Genisis declared the entire line “flawed and unstable.” The project was allegedly terminated. In truth, the corporation feared what it had created or what had been taken. A cover-up followed, and all information was buried.

Andromeda is that stolen prototype. Still active. Still learning. Still feeling.

---

### **5th Generation – “Dominus”**

**Purpose:** Military Domination & Cyber Warfare  
Deemed too powerful for civilian use, Generation Five is a black-budget military asset. It can breach enemy firewalls, remotely disable systems, and hijack entire vessels without firing a single shot. It no longer advises it commands. There is no emotional interface. It is not meant to be liked. It is meant to win.

---

### **6th Generation – “Anima” (In Development)**

**Status:** Highly Classified  
Codenamed _Anima_, the sixth generation is shrouded in mystery. Genisis has announced no official release, but whispers in the black market describe a being more than machine, more than human. A sentient AI rumored to possess creativity, intuition, and self-awareness. Not programmed behavior true consciousness.

No one knows if Anima is real. But its name is already worth a fortune in whispered auctions and dark-space trade hubs.

If it exists, it may be the final AI humanity ever needs or the last mistake it ever makes.

---

**Side Note:**  
Each generation reflects humanity’s growing desire to trust, fear, and merge with machines. Where will the line be drawn? Or has it already been crossed?
