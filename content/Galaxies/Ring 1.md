Ring 1 is the innermost of the three [[Ring Galaxies]] and once considered the crown jewel of the known universe. It is the smallest of the rings, but also the most technologically advanced.

Many believed Ring 1 to be a utopia. The environment was bright, stable, and ideal for scientific progress. At its heart flowed a unique energy field, later identified as [[Virellion]] the pure essence of the ancient [[Aethar]].

## Unique Features

- **Energy Field**: Devices in Ring 1 did not need traditional power. The atmosphere itself recharged machines and supported continuous technological operation.
- **Civilization**: The [[Lua]], a highly advanced species, became the protectors and caretakers of this ring.
- **Function**: Designed as a beacon of progress and balance, Ring 1 served as the scientific and philosophical center of the ring civilization.

## Current Status

Today, Ring 1 is a dead galaxy. Cold, hollow, and without life.

The disaster began when the [[Lua]] tried to access [[Virellion]], unaware that it was the spiritual essence of the [[Aethar]]. Their experiments tore open the protective field that kept the [[World Devourer]] out of the rings.

Unable to stop the invasion, the Lua sacrificed themselves in a desperate final act. They unleashed an unknown weapon and destroyed the [[Ring Gates]] leading to Ring 1, preventing the Devourer from spreading further.

## Legacy

Though no known life remains in Ring 1, some believe that echoes of the Lua and fragments of the Aethar consciousness still linger in its forgotten structures.
