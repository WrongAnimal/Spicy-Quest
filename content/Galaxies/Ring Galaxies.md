The galaxy of _Spicy Quest_ consists of three artificially created rings that lie within one another, each governed by its own physical laws. They were created by the [[Aethar]], an ancient civilization from the true galaxy, long before recorded history.

The rings serve as the last safe haven for life in the universe. Outside of them, everything has fallen into darkness and chaos. The Aethar are seen as creator gods of today’s world, even though their true existence is only known through legend.

## Structure of the Ring Galaxies

### Ring 1 – The Inner Ring

The smallest but technologically most significant ring  
It was considered the most beautiful and radiant region ever created

**Special Trait**: It contains an infinite energy source drawn directly from the atmosphere  
**Effect**: Technology worked without fuel or electricity. Devices charged themselves  
**Civilization**: The [[Lua]], an extremely advanced culture, became the guardians of this ring

### Ring 2 – The Middle Ring

Little is known but due to its stable conditions, it is home to many civilizations  
Functions and characteristics to be added

### Ring 3 – The Outer Ring

The largest ring  
It holds most of the known planets and trade routes

## Ring Connections

All rings are connected through so-called [[Ring Gates]] which fold space and time  
These gates allow travel between the rings although most of them are now destroyed or deactivated
