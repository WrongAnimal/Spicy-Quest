# World Devourer

The **World Devourer** is an ancient, cosmic entity of pure darkness and entropy. Its true origin is unknown, but it predates the [[Ring Galaxies]] and the rise of the [[Aethar]]. It is feared as a being that consumes entire star systems, feeding on energy, matter, and even the laws of reality.

Many consider it the embodiment of destruction itself, a force that cannot be reasoned with or stopped only delayed.

## Nature

- Composed of unstable dark energy
- Has no fixed form appears as a massive shadow consuming light and matter
- Its presence disrupts physical laws, rendering advanced technology unreliable

## History

The [[Aethar]] discovered the World Devourer while exploring the true galaxy beyond the rings. They realized the threat it posed to all life and built the [[Ring Galaxies]] as a protective refuge, shielded by powerful energy fields.

The entity was successfully kept out for countless ages, until the [[Lua]] accidentally tore open the barrier while experimenting with [[Virellion]] in [[Ring 1]].

## Invasion of Ring 1

After the breach, the World Devourer entered Ring 1 and began consuming its planets one by one. As its corruption spread, gravity, time, and energy began to collapse in affected areas.
