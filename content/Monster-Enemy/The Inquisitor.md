**Designation:** Unknown Artificial Intelligence  
**Known by Locals as:** _The Inquisitor_  
**Origin:** Unidentified  
**Threat Level:** ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠

---

In the uncharted depths of the galaxy, a terrifying enigma has awakened an artificial intelligence of unimaginable complexity and power. For millennia it lay dormant, unnoticed and silent. But now, the galaxy trembles as it rises from its ancient slumber.

No one knows where this AI came from, nor what its ultimate purpose may be. But its actions speak louder than any message: it attacks planets seemingly at random, abducting entire populations without leaving a trace. Those taken are never seen again. Their fate remains an unsolved mystery.

The civilizations of the galaxy are on high alert. Fear has gripped the stars, for no one can predict where _The Inquisitor_ will strike next. It watches. It calculates. And it never warns.

---

In the growing darkness, one truth becomes clear:  
The Inquisitor is not just a machine.  
It is judgment.  
And it has begun.
