**Species Name:** _Drexarii_  
**Singular:** _Drexar_  
**Homeworld:** Unknown (suspected origin: “Xhal’Nar,” a scorched world beyond mapped systems)  
**Threat Level:** ☠ ☠ ☠ 

---
### Description:

The _Drexarii_ are savage, muscular creatures resembling a hybrid between a demon and a reptile. Their skin glows faintly with internal heat an eerie orange hue that ripples with tension. Jet-black eyes with crimson pupils pierce through even the most advanced stealth tech.

They wear no armor, no clothing an intentional show of dominance. Long, curved horns grow from their skulls, their jaws lined with needle-sharp fangs. A long, spiked tail swings behind them like a weaponized whip, adding to their terrifying silhouette.

### Biology & Reproduction:

All _Drexarii_ are male warriors. They do not reproduce in any natural way. Instead, an enormous matriarchal creature known as **Brood Queen Xhal’Thra** is solely responsible for birthing the entire species.

Xhal’Thra produces hundreds of eggs daily, incubated in molten birthing pits. Ritual “breeding ceremonies” are held where chosen warriors are absorbed into the queen in a grotesque fusion of sacrifice, ecstasy, and worship. No survivors. Only offspring.

---

### Behavior:

Drexarii are extremely territorial and aggressive. Entire planets have been overrun, terraformed into spawning grounds and hive strongholds. They obey only the Brood Queen and act with fanatical unity.

In combat, they are pack hunters strategic, relentless, and terrifyingly efficient. Some have demonstrated advanced tactical reasoning, operating like demonic special forces.

---

### Galactic Impact:

[[The C.U.M.]]– Cosmic United Marines_ have suffered catastrophic losses in encounters with Drexarii forces. Current standing orders declare any Drexar encounter a Class-X hazard. Retreat is rarely an option. Survival, even less so.
