**Species Name:**  Giga
**Threat Level:** ☠ ☠ ☠ 

---

In the depths of the universe exists a species known as the **Giga** colossal and terrifying beings whose mere presence inspires fear and devastation. With a lifespan of over **950 years** and reaching towering heights of up to **800 meters**, Gigas are among the most massive lifeforms ever encountered. However, their intimidating size is overshadowed by their **lack of intelligence**, as they possess no distinct language or developed culture.

### Communication

Gigas communicate solely through **primitive sounds and roars**, lacking any known linguistic structure. Their interactions with other beings are overwhelmingly hostile and often end in **violence and destruction**.

### Culture and Intelligence

Despite their longevity and physical prowess, Gigas have **no known culture, traditions, or technologies**. They live solitary, nomadic lives, guided by instinct and aggression rather than reason or cooperation.

### Abilities

- **Extreme physical strength** and durability
    
- **Immune to environmental hazards**, including vacuum and toxic atmospheres
    
- **Able to survive on lifeless worlds** with no breathable air
    
- **Absorb energy from surroundings** by converting atoms into usable oxygen
    

### Diet

Gigas feed on virtually anything they can get their hands on, including **rocks, trees**, and entire **structures**. Their digestive systems are incredibly adaptable, allowing them to extract nutrients from non-organic matter.

### Reproduction

Contrary to older theories, Gigas reproduce **naturally** like most species. In a unique twist of biology and behavior, it is the **females who compete** for male attention.

During mating seasons, **female Gigas engage in violent combat** to establish dominance and impress potential mates. These titanic duels are cataclysmic events, often lasting days and reshaping the terrain around them.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Witnessing a Giga mating contest is extremely dangerous. Observers should maintain orbital distance seismic shockwaves have been known to damage low-flying spacecraft.
